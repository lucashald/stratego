# Dispatch 02 — the Weirgate

Campaign turn 2. Treasury: 15 crowns.

## Where things stand

Vare took Cassewick and burned the winter stores. The vanguard that did it is
spent — one rider, Distress, limped back east. But the raid was never the point;
it was the tide feeling ahead of itself. Now the **main host** comes on behind it,
and it is nothing like the vanguard: heavy, slow, and big, marching for the
**Weirgate** — the fortified sluice-and-causeway that carries the south road over
the millwater and into Halgate's own country. Hold the Weirgate and the host is
stalled at the water. Lose it and the road to the city is open.

This is a fight to **hold a fortified point** against weight — the mirror of
Cassewick's fast raid. It is the battle the Gate Guard was built for: a wall with
heavy bows behind it, on ground where being slow does not matter because you are
not going anywhere.

## Known enemy force — the heavy host

Scouts count six formations, and **Bulwark is leading it** — the Vare Heavy that
watched Ashmere break at the bridge and has been carrying the tide's word ever
since. It came back at the head of an army:

- **Bulwark** — Heavy Infantry (8). Leads.
- **Sessions** — Heavy Infantry (8).
- **Assize** — Heavy Archer (7). Their answer to your bows, and a real one.
- **Mandate** — Medium Infantry (7).
- **Sergeant** — Medium Infantry (7).
- **Amerce** — Medium Cavalry (7). The one fast thing, to turn a flank.

Heavier than anything Halgate has faced — roughly 44 Strength to a single Halgate
company's ~34. But you are the defender, on the causeway, and the causeway is
worth more than the eight points of Strength you give away.

## The decision

**Send the Gate Guard (the wall).** Fresh, untouched, five heavy formations with
two heavy bows — Portcullis, Bastion, Halberd, Sable, Sterling. This is its
battle: plant it on the causeway and let the host break on it. The cost is that
you commit your elite core; if it bleeds here, the city's own Guard is what bled.

**Send the Charter Company (the remnant).** Three formations — Errant (at 1),
Quill, Vellum — fast but battered, and horse-and-bow is the wrong tool against a
wall of heavies you cannot choose the terms against. You would be reinforcing and
hiring heavily to make it viable, and even then you would be fighting a slugging
match your company is built to avoid. Keeps the Guard safe, at real risk to the
fight.

The honest read: the Weirgate wants the Guard. But committing your best is its own
kind of exposure, and the Charter Company still needs rebuilding either way.

at (Strength) + 2:

- **Gate Guard** to full: Bastion (+1), Sterling (+1) = 2 crowns. Cheap to top off.
- **Charter Company** to rebuild: Errant is at 1 (up to +5 = 5 crowns), and it is
  three formations — you would likely hire as well to field a real company.
- Reserve to hire: Heavy Infantry (10), Medium Archer (8), Light Cavalry (8).
