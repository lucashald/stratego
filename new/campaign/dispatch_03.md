# Dispatch 03 — the initiative changes hands

Campaign turn 3. Treasury: 13 crowns.

## Where things stand

Vare's heavy host is destroyed and Bulwark is dead. For the first time in the war,
the tide is not the thing advancing — Halgate is. The Weirgate held, the road to
the city is shut, and the enemy is on its back foot with its main column gone.

This is the moment to take something back. **Cassewick** still flies Vare colours:
after the raid, a thin garrison holds the granary town and the burned stores, and
Distress — the lone survivor of the vanguard, the raider that won the town in the
first place — is still there. But Vare will not give it up cheaply. The moment
Halgate marches on Cassewick, a **relief column** starts for it from the east, and
whoever holds the town when the dust settles keeps it.

So this one is a **race against a relief clock**, and it is Halgate on the
offensive — the harder posture, because Halgate's companies are built to hold, not
to storm. Retake Cassewick and hold it before the relief arrives, and the
campaign's momentum is yours. Arrive too slow, or storm it and get caught by fresh
troops, and you have spent a company to take nothing.

## The decision

**Which company — and this time offence is the question.**

- **Rebuild the Charter Company and send it.** Fast, and speed is what beats a
  relief clock — get there first, take the town, hold it against troops still
  arriving. But it is a remnant: Errant (at 1), Quill, Vellum. You would spend
  real coin reinforcing and hiring to make it a company again, and it is the right
  tool for a race.
- **Send the Gate Guard.** It just won, but it is slow and down Bastion, and a
  race to seize a town is the exact fight that punished slowness at Cassewick the
  first time. Wrong tool, proven army.

## A real opponent, if you want one

Two battles running, you have noted the bot plays too passively — and the debriefs
agree: a static fight against it is a shooting gallery. A counterattack with a
relief clock is the kind of scenario where that matters least, because the bot can
win just by getting its relief column onto the objective in time. But if you want
a genuine contest, **I can command Vare myself for this one** — the counterattack
on Cassewick is a natural marquee battle for it, and a pursuit/relief race is
exactly the thing the bot can't run. Say the word and I take the field as Red
instead of the bot.
